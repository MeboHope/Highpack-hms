# Phase 27 — Homepage Full Platform Journey

## Purpose
The HighPark homepage is now designed as a long-form guided platform journey. Visitors can scroll downward through the homepage and encounter the major public experiences, customer journey, owner operations, administrator role, support pages and AI assistant before opening the full destination.

## Added
- Platform journey overview immediately after the hero.
- Page-by-page experience previews for marketplace, property details, customer workspace, owner workspace, and support pages.
- Role-aware platform section for Customer, Property Owner and Administrator.
- HighPark AI Assistant showcase section.
- Direct CTA links to the existing public routes.
- Protected customer/owner/admin areas intentionally route through sign-in rather than exposing private screens to unauthenticated visitors.
- Existing animated featured-property marquee, asset-aware categories, statistics, locations, testimonials and CTA are preserved.

## Important
This does not duplicate private dashboard pages inside the public homepage. Instead, it gives visitors a premium visual preview of what each area provides and links them into the existing authenticated route. This avoids leaking private data and keeps the homepage fast and maintainable.
